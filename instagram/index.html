Platform Rules

Instagram Username Rules (2026)

📏 Max 30 Characters

Letters, numbers, periods, and underscores only — no spaces or other symbols.

🆔 Username ≠ Name

Your "Name" field can have spaces and emoji. Your @username can't — keep both consistent for search.

🔄 Change Anytime

No cooldown like TikTok — but the moment you drop a handle, someone else can claim it.

🔡 Case-Insensitive

Instagram treats "Luna.Bakes" and "luna.bakes" as the same account when people search for you.
