How to Generate Lorem Ipsum Text Online – Step by Step

📅 2026-06-15⏱ 8 min read✍ ToolsNowPro TeamUpdated 2026-07-23

If you need placeholder text quickly, the easiest method is a Lorem Ipsum generator instead of copying the same sentence over and over. It gives you a cleaner mockup, and it saves the time you'd otherwise spend manually padding out a text block to the right length.

The process itself is simple — decide how much text you need, generate it, and paste it in — but getting real value out of it depends on a few details: matching the length to the section, adapting the approach for wireframes versus polished mockups, and knowing when to stop using placeholder text altogether.

Step 1: Decide the text length

Start with the size of the section you're filling, not a generic default. A hero block might only need a headline and one short line. A blog layout may need two or three paragraphs. A pricing table needs just a few words per cell. Matching the length to the layout is what makes the draft feel like a real page instead of a placeholder shell — a wireframe with one sentence of filler in a spot meant to hold 200 words will hide exactly the layout problems you're trying to catch.

Step 2: Generate the placeholder copy

Use the free online generator to create the text in seconds — short snippets for cards and labels, longer paragraphs for article previews, or enough content to fill a full page layout. Generating a few reusable lengths (short, medium, long) up front is faster than regenerating text block by block as you build out the page.

Step 3: Paste it into your mockup and check both layouts

Copy the output into your prototype, CMS draft, or design file, then preview it — and check it on both desktop and mobile, not just the screen you're designing on. A headline that looks balanced on a wide screen can turn cramped the moment the body copy underneath wraps onto more lines on a phone, and that's exactly the kind of problem placeholder text is meant to surface early.

Using it for wireframes vs. polished mockups

The two use cases call for slightly different text. For a rough wireframe, the goal is pure structure testing — keep it neutral and don't worry about tone, since nobody is meant to read it closely yet. For a polished client-facing mockup, it helps to generate text in the rough length and paragraph rhythm of what the final copy will be, so the visual weight of the page during review matches what will actually ship.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Text that's too short — a two-word placeholder in a spot meant for a paragraph tells you nothing about wrapping or spacing.
  • Text that's too long — an oversized block makes the design look heavier than the real content ever will.
  • Mixing placeholder styles — Lorem Ipsum in one section and improvised real sentences in another makes the page read as inconsistent during review.
  • Leaving it in too long — placeholder text left in a near-final design reads as unfinished, even when the layout itself is done.
