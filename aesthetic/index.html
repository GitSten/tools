Style Guide

5 Aesthetic Username Styles Explained

🌸 Soft Girl

Pastel, sweet and dreamy. Keywords: petal, cloud, mochi, honey, velvet, rose, bloom.

🖤 Dark Academia

Gothic, literary and moody. Keywords: thorn, raven, dusk, obsidian, void, crypt, ink.

🌿 Cottagecore

Rural, earthy and whimsical. Keywords: moss, fern, bloom, river, oak, clover, meadow.

🌙 Celestial

Cosmic and mystical. Keywords: luna, comet, nebula, eclipse, aurora, stardust, solstice.

◻ Minimalist

Clean, one or two syllables. Single-word names: veil, mist, haze, drift, form, tone, still.
